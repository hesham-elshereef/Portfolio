About Me

A little bit about me

I'm a final-year Electronics and Communications Engineering student with a clear focus on DevOps and cloud infrastructure. My interest in this field grew out of a simple curiosity about what happens after code is written — how it actually gets built, shipped, and kept running reliably — and that curiosity has shaped most of my training and projects since.

Over the past year I've worked hands-on with Linux administration, containerization, and infrastructure automation through the DevOps Engineer Track at DEPI and a software engineering program at ITI, alongside AWS cloud training through NTI and a CCNA foundation in networking. I like understanding systems end to end, from the network layer up through the application, and I enjoy the discipline of automating things properly rather than doing them manually once and moving on.

What makes me different is that I don't stop at making a command or a container work. I focus on understanding how the system behaves, applying least-privilege access, and testing both the success and the failure cases, so what I build is secure, documented, and ready to be reproduced.

I'm currently looking for an internship or entry-level DevOps role where I can take this foundation and apply it to real infrastructure, learn from experienced engineers, and keep building toward production-level responsibility.
